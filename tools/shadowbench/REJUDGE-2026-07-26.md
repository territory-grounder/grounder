# Re-judge of the archived head-to-head under symmetric inputs (roadmap P1-4)

**Date:** 2026-07-26 · **Rubric:** `core/judge/rubric.json` sha256 `0be4d8f697d9` · **Judge model:** `primary`
· **Pairs:** the archived set, re-judged with deterministic per-pair A/B seeds · **judge_unavailable:** 0/15

## Why this was run

TG was losing the blind head-to-head **20–0**. Before funding weeks of work to close that gap, the question
worth answering was: **how much of it did the harness create?** Three input-side defects were found and fixed
(MR !567 and the winner rule below); this re-judges the *same pairs* with the *same rubric* so the delta is
attributable to the harness rather than asserted.

## Headline: the gap is REAL. It survives symmetric inputs.

| Configuration | pred | tie | TG |
|---|---|---|---|
| Original (asymmetric inputs, pooled winner) | 14 | 1 | **0** |
| Symmetric inputs, pooled winner | 12 | 1 | 2 |
| **Symmetric inputs + like-for-like winner** | **13** | **2** | **0** |

**On strictly comparable ground TG did not beat the predecessor in a single one of the 15 pairs.**

The harness asymmetry accounts for roughly **0.18 of a 1.28-point gap — about 14%.** The remaining ~86% is a
real capability difference. `evidence_grounded` did **not** improve despite TG's grounding now being visible,
which is the most informative single result here: TG's trajectory was absent from the comparison, and showing
it did not rescue that dimension.

## ⚠️ Two ways the published numbers were WRONG IN TG'S FAVOUR

1. **The published overall (4.667 vs 4.292) pooled a one-sided dimension.** `falsifiable_prediction` is scored
   for TG (mean 5.0) and **null for the predecessor**, which structurally commits no predictions. The two
   figures were means over different dimension sets. **The true like-for-like gap is ~3× what was published**
   (1.10 vs 0.375).
2. **Both of TG's apparent wins were awarded on that same one-sided dimension.** The judge's own reasons said
   so — *"a strong falsifiable prediction"*, *"an exemplary falsifiable prediction"* — while TG led on no
   dimension both systems were scored on. With the like-for-like winner rule those become **pred** and
   **tie**.

## ⚠️ The sample is far weaker than "21 pairs" implies

The 21 archived comparisons are **15 unique (predecessor, TG) combinations** drawn from just **6 predecessor
incidents** across 6 hosts. One incident (`IFRNLLEI01PRD-1851`) accounts for **7 of them**.

Clustering by predecessor incident — the real independent unit:

|---|---|---|

Predecessor better in **5 of 6** clusters, TG better in **0**. Exact sign test on n=6: **p = 0.031**.
Cluster-mean Δ = **−1.370**.

Suggestive, not conclusive. `librespeed01` is also among the hosts, and the predecessor is known to recognise
it — a contamination the pair supply must exclude.

## What changed in the harness

- **TG's card carried a conclusion in the field labelled `trajectory`** while the predecessor's carried its
  full reasoning. TG's real ReAct transcript (`agent_step`, 2,502 steps / 415 sessions) was never extracted.
  Now shipped, attempt-bounded (103 refs have duplicate cycle numbers; a naive aggregate would interleave
  multiple attempts).
- **One shared character budget**, applied identically to both cards, conclusion-first so truncation costs
  supporting detail rather than the verdict.
- **OVERALL and the WINNER are now decided on comparable dimensions only.** One-sided dimensions are still
  reported — as unilateral properties, which is what they are.

## What this means for the roadmap

**P5 (close the diagnosis gap) is funded by evidence, not assumption.** The gap is real, it is concentrated in
`correct_diagnosis` (+1.400) and `evidence_grounded` (+1.333), and it is not a harness artifact.

**But no exceed-proof may be published on this sample.** n=6 clusters, one host contaminated, and every number
here predates the P2 identity repair. The confirmatory campaign needs ≥30 fault-class-matched pairs from ≥15
hosts, ≤3 per host, pre-registered before the first judged pair.
